Noor Quran Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

About Noor Quran

Noor Quran is a Quran reading app.

Information stored on your device

Noor Quran does not require an account, has no backend, and does not provide cloud sync. Quran bookmarks, your last-read position, and app settings and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Noor Quran uses Google AdMob ads. Depending on your consent and the Google services available on your device, AdMob may process device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising data.

Ads are not shown inside the Quran reader or over Mushaf/Quran pages.

Your choices

You can manage privacy options from the app settings when required.

Sharing and sale of personal data

We do not sell personal data.
