HomeRep Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

About HomeRep

HomeRep is a workout app.

Information stored on your device

HomeRep does not require an account, has no backend, and does not provide cloud sync. Your workout history is stored locally on your device. Activity Rings use this local workout history.

Optional device features

The steps sensor is used only if implemented in the app. Foreground location is used only for user-started Walk/Run sessions if implemented. These features are not used outside their stated purposes.

Advertising

HomeRep uses Google AdMob ads. Depending on your consent and the Google services available on your device, AdMob may process device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising data.

Ads are not shown during active workouts, timers, rest screens, or near workout control buttons.

Sharing and sale of personal data

We do not sell personal data.
