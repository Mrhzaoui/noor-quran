Noor Quran Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

About Noor Quran

Noor Quran is a Quran reading app. No user account is required to use the app. Noor Quran has no backend and does not sync data to the cloud.

Information stored on your device

Your Quran bookmarks, last-read position, and app settings and preferences are stored locally on your device.

You can remove this local app data by clearing Noor Quran's app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising

Noor Quran uses Google AdMob to show ads on selected non-reading screens. Depending on your consent and the Google services available on your device, AdMob may process device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising-related data.

Ads are not shown inside the Quran reader or over Mushaf/Quran pages.

Privacy options

You can manage privacy options from Noor Quran's app Settings when required.

Sharing and sale of personal data

Noor Quran does not sell personal data.
